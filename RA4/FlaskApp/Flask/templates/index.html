Equipment Analysis Dashboard

Predicting daily drone attrition through combined arms analysis

Dataset Information

This application analyzes daily equipment losses in the Russia-Ukraine conflict. The target variable is whether the daily variation in drone losses is above the median (High) or not (Low).
